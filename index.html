Academic Homepage

PhD candidate working at the intersection of AI-assisted language learning, EFL writing, and learner psychology.

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Linguistics at the Department of Foreign Languages and Literatures, Tsinghua University. My research examines how modern technologies, including AI-assisted learning environments and digital writing tools, shape learners’ motivation, strategy use, engagement, and reflective development in English as a foreign language context.

Before joining Tsinghua University, I completed an M.A. in Applied Linguistics in Foreign Languages at Beijing Language and Culture University and a B.A. in English Language and Literature at Sichuan International Studies University.

Ongoing work

Current Research Agenda

My current research agenda focuses on active second language learning in technology-mediated environments, especially in EFL writing. I am interested in how learners proactively seek resources, interact with feedback, evaluate learning strategies, and construct productive relationships with AI-supported tools.

This line of work connects applied linguistics with educational psychology and human-computer interaction by asking how language learners become more agentic, reflective, and strategically adaptive in digital learning contexts.
